**Link:**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00336

2. **The Deterministic Horizon: When Extended Reasoning Fails and Tool Delegation Becomes Necessary**

**Publish Date:**
2026-06-02

**一句话总结:**
该论文通过注意力瓶颈定理与状态空间Jaccard指标，在12个模型和8个任务域（包括SWE-Bench）上实证发现确定性状态跟踪任务中存在确定性边界（d*∈[19,31]），工具集成推理准确率达86-94%，远超纯推理的24-42%，为智能体系统何时转向混合方法提供准则。

**Link:**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00376

3. **MOSAIC: Modular Orchestration for Structured Agentic Intelligence and Composition**

2026-06-02

4. **Relational Intervention During Functional Collapse in Large Language Models: A Lexical-Statistical Ablation and a Structure x Register Factorial**

**Publish Date:**
2026-06-02

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00935
